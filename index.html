About the grill

A neighbourhood grill house on Norfolk Street.

Charcoal Grill has been firing up the grill on Norfolk Street for King's Lynn locals who know the difference between fast food and proper food cooked fast. Kebabs hand-prepped, meats marinated, pizzas pulled from a stone-hot oven — nothing fancy, just made well and made hot.

We're a takeaway first. Walk in, ring through your order or tap it in on Foodhub. We'll have it ready when you're ready to eat — whether that's a Wednesday teatime or a Friday after the pubs close.

What we're known for

  • Char-grilled doner & shish kebabs
  • Hot stone-baked pizzas to order
  • Flame-grilled chicken & meat plates
  • Open late for after-hours appetites
